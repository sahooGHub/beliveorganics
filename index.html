Where we begin

A belief before a blueprint.

Be.Live did not begin with large funding, institutional backing, or a claim to have all the answers. It began with a question: can healthier food systems and stronger farming communities grow through awareness, learning and participation?

We are not presenting a finished model. We are learning, adapting, experimenting and sharing—and inviting others to shape the journey with us.

Not built on expertise.
Not built on wealth.
Built on learning, collaboration and belief.

The missing connection

Food is more than what’s on a plate.

Consumers want food they can trust. Farmers need knowledge and fair opportunities. Useful research and practical wisdom are scattered, while many communities have grown distant from the land that feeds them. Be.Live aims to help reconnect these parts.

People

Informed consumers and curious learners who want to participate.

Food

More transparent relationships with what we grow, buy and eat.

Knowledge

Learning gathered from research, practitioners and farm experience.

Farmers & nature

Stronger communities and farming practices attentive to the living landscape.

Awareness→Informed choices→Market demand→Better practices→Healthier outcomes

Rooted in place

Why Odisha?

We see Odisha as a place to learn and experiment at a practical scale, with agricultural heritage, access to Bhubaneswar and Cuttack markets, and room to build long-term relationships. This is a direction to explore—not a promise that every challenge is solved.

Odisha: a place to build

Potential for practical experimentation, learning with local communities, and developing agricultural models suited to the region.

Bengaluru: a vital market

A strong consumption market and a useful source of ideas and partnerships. Our current thinking is that Odisha may offer a more fitting base for ecosystem-building.

The returning generation

Contribution is bigger than funding.

Many people left villages for education, work and opportunity. Some now want to give back. That contribution can take many forms: knowledge, mentoring, networks, time, volunteering, ideas or funding. We are looking for collaborators, not just followers.

Village roots → Education → Experience → Giving back → Community impact

Not a large corporationNot claiming certaintyNot a quick-profit promiseNot a finished project
