Research sources & attribution

This page collects primary-source starting points for organisation profiles and programme descriptions discussed in the Knowledge Garden. Organisation websites are useful sources for what an organisation says about its own work; their figures should be labelled as organisation-reported, not independently verified.

Editorial note: A source link does not by itself prove every claim on the research page. Check that the specific source supports the specific number or statement, record the source date, and qualify or remove claims that cannot be confirmed. Do not imply endorsement or partnership.

Akshayakalpa

Official site: akshayakalpa.org

About: About Us

Social and community impact: Social & Community Impact

Use for: organisation background and its own description of farmer and community programmes. The current research-page figure “750+ farmer families” should only remain if a source directly supporting that number is located; otherwise remove the exact count or clearly cite a dated independent report.

Bhoomi Network

Official site: bhoominetwork.org

Programs: Bhoomi Programs

Eco Village: Bhoomi Eco Village, Gumlapuram

Use for: the network’s stated mission and activities. Avoid unsupported claims about annual festivals, nationwide reach, or network scale unless a specific source page confirms them.

Andhra Pradesh Community Managed Natural Farming (APCNF)

Official programme site: apcnf.in

Use for: current programme name, objectives and programme information. For state targets, participant numbers or results, cite a specific dated APCNF/government report rather than relying on a general description. Distinguish APCNF from claims about Subhash Palekar personally.

Subhash Palekar / Zero Budget Natural Farming

Practitioner website: Palekar Zero Budget Spiritual Farming

Use for: attribution of the approach and the practitioner’s own materials. Verify farmer adoption numbers and government programme targets independently through dated official records before publishing.

Save Soil / Conscious Planet

Campaign site: Conscious Planet

FAQ: Save Soil FAQs

Use for: campaign positions and campaign-reported reach. Clearly identify these as campaign statements, and use independent scientific sources for general soil-health claims.

Navdanya

Official site: navdanya.org

Community seed banks: Community Seed Bank

Overview: Navdanya Overview

Use for: Navdanya’s own description of its seed-bank work and the figures it reports. The current research text’s figures (5,000 rice varieties, 150+ wheat varieties, and thousands of others) do not match all current official page figures; replace them with a specific, consistently sourced statement or omit the numbers.

Kheti Virasat Mission

Official site: khetivirasatmission.org

Mission: Mission

Organic farming: Organic Farming

Use for: the organisation’s stated work. Claims about farmer health, cancer, suicides, input-cost reductions or numbers reached need carefully matched, reliable evidence; avoid causal medical claims without strong independent sources.

How we cite

For example: “Navdanya reports operating community seed banks in multiple states (Navdanya, Community Seed Bank, accessed [date]).” Add a publication date where available. Use a second, independent source for consequential numbers or scientific conclusions.

This source list is a research aid, not a legal review or independent verification of every profile statement.
